# Show List visual system
Personal screening notebook, mode Operate. Dark graphite for evening viewing; lime accent marks actions, not availability claims. Self-hosted Manrope headings and DM Sans text. Real TMDB posters anchor a compact catalog. No decorative hero, metric cards, dashboard sidebar, or invented reviews.
Seven grounded candidates: repertory cinema program; library hold shelf; DVD collection spines; TV listings; festival screening grid; edit-suite bin; personal screening notebook. Assigned candidate seven. Challenger judgments: tensegrity declined (clarity of distinct states retained); deep dive declined (progressive detail retained); transit competitive for wayfinding but weaker audience fit; pixorama declined (consistent grid retained); flight instruments declined (signal hierarchy retained); HyperCard competitive for editing but weaker movie recognition. Chosen notebook is raised by explicit distinct states, concise signal hierarchy, consistent grid, and focused detail.
First viewport: brand and sheet link, compact task heading with Add a title, watch-status tabs, search and filters, six columns of posters. Phone: two columns, wrapping filters, fully readable labels. Primary path: filter → detail → service or watch status. Signature: poster hover reveals detail action; details slide in as a focused reading panel. Reduced-motion disables animated transitions. New title entry is a focused form; failed saves keep entered values.
Workflow: user approved code-first and delegated implementation. No further design-choice round needed under the user’s approval and Sites build guidance.
